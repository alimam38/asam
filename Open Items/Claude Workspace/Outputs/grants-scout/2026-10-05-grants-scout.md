## 2. CLIR — Digitizing Hidden Collections: Amplifying Unheard Voices (Cycle 5, Mellon-funded)
- **What it funds:** $50K–$300K to digitize rare and unique materials that deepen public understanding of underrepresented communities. Turner angle: AME and Bishop Henry McNeal Turner holdings, plus seminary records since 1894. This fits the "Turner Digital Archive" concept already in the pipeline as a *new funder* for it.
- **Deadline:** initial application **2026-10-20** (15 days out; time not posted). Invited finalists are notified Feb 2027.
- **Eligibility:** applicants must be US 501(c)(3) nonprofits that *collect, preserve and share rare and unique materials with the general public*. Partnerships are allowed. No accreditation bar is stated. **Flag:** the applicant must be a collecting organization with public access. If Turner's archive is not open to the public on a regular schedule, the route is to partner with a collecting institution (for example AUC Woodruff Library, which holds ITC/AME materials). **Verify:** confirm what Turner actually holds and its access policy before spending any hours. A tight window with the WIP cap at 2 makes this a go/no-go decision this week.
