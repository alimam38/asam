# Nonprofit/education pricing digest — 2026-08-01

For Turner Theological Seminary (501(c)(3), accredited seminary) + Plumbline dev work.
Method: built-in web search + SerpAPI precision layer (4/6 calls used). All 5 items verified on vendor pages this run; none in seen index.

## 1. Claude for Nonprofits (Anthropic)
- **What you get:** Up to 75% off Claude Team and Enterprise plans, plus nonprofit connectors (Benevity, Blackbaud, Candid) and a free "AI Fluency for Nonprofits" course. Team normally $25–30/user/mo, so ~$7/user/mo at full discount.
- **Eligibility/validation:** Registered nonprofits; apply at claude.com/solutions/nonprofits (announced Dec 2025).
- **Source:** https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-nonprofits

## 2. OpenAI for Nonprofits (ChatGPT Team/Enterprise)
- **What you get:** ChatGPT Team/Business at $8/user/mo (annual) or $10/user/mo (monthly) — vs ~$25–30 standard; up to 75% off Enterprise.
- **Eligibility/validation:** 501(c)(3) (or intl equivalent); verify via **Goodstack**, then OpenAI application review.
- **Source:** https://goodstack.org/software-discounts/openai

## 3. Cloudflare Project Galileo
- **What you get:** Free Business-plan-level security stack: unmetered DDoS mitigation, WAF, CDN, DNS, SSL, Zero Trust access, Cloudflare Workers — useful for both the seminary site and Plumbline hosting/security.
- **Eligibility/validation:** Public-interest / civil-society orgs (arts, human rights, journalism, community orgs). Apply directly with domain + mission + nonprofit status, or get sponsored by one of 40+ partner orgs. Mission-based review — a seminary should apply as a community/civil-society nonprofit; approval not guaranteed.
- **Source:** https://www.cloudflare.com/galileo/

## 4. Okta for Good (identity & security)
- **What you get:** 50 free Workforce Identity licenses (SSO/MFA) + 50% off additional licenses, 50% off Auth0 plans (relevant for Plumbline auth), 50% off advanced security add-ons, free Okta Learning courses.
- **Eligibility/validation:** Valid 501(c)(3) verified via **Goodstack**. Note: "schools" are listed as ineligible — apply under the seminary's 501(c)(3) nonprofit identity, not as an educational institution.
- **Source:** https://www.okta.com/industries/nonprofits/

## 5. PCs for People — Tech for Nonprofits (hardware)
- **What you get:** Low-cost refurbished laptops/desktops + tech support; small orders (≤5) via pcsrefurbished.com with eligibility docs at checkout, bulk (6+) via partner@pcsforpeople.org. Closes the hardware gap flagged in the 2026-06-23 run.
- **Eligibility/validation:** 501(c)(3) orgs **or accredited educational institutions** — the seminary qualifies on both tracks. Upload IRS 501(c)(3) letter or accreditation docs. Note: no Office 2019 machines or hotspots for internal org use.
- **Source:** https://www.pcsforpeople.org/nonprofits/
