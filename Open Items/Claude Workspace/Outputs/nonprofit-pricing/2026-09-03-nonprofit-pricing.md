# Nonprofit/education pricing digest — 2026-09-03

Scope: Turner Theological Seminary (501(c)(3), accredited seminary) + Plumbline dev work.
Deduped against seen-index. SerpAPI unavailable this run (no key at Dropbox /Claude/.env) — built-in search only.

## 1. Box for Nonprofits — 10 free licenses + 50% off Enterprise
- **What you get:** Up to 10 perpetual donated Box Starter licenses (secure cloud storage/file sharing), 50% off any Box Enterprise plan, 75% off add-ons. Covers the security/backup gap cheaply.
- **Eligibility/validation:** 501(c)(3) verified via a free TechSoup Validation Token (2–5 business days). Listed exclusions include hospitals and *schools* — apply on the 501(c)(3) nonprofit track, not as an educational institution.
- **Source:** https://www.box.org/use-box

## 2. Asana for Nonprofits — 50% off forever
- **What you get:** 50% off Starter or Advanced (Enterprise via sales). Starter ≈ $10.99/user/mo → ~$5.50; discount persists as long as you stay.
- **Eligibility/validation:** 501(c)(3) **and** higher-education institutions explicitly eligible. Verification via Goodstack; discount code emailed after approval.
- **Source:** https://asana.com/industry/nonprofit

## 3. Airtable — 50% off Team plan (education track)
- **What you get:** 50% per-user monthly discount on Team plans (~$20/user/mo → ~$10). Good low-code database/ops layer.
- **Eligibility/validation:** ⚠️ The *nonprofit* track excludes religious organizations — apply on the **education** track instead: accredited not-for-profit institutions qualify with an accreditation certification. Does not apply to Business/Enterprise.
- **Source:** https://support.airtable.com/articles/4920135568-applying-for-non-profit-education-and-student-airtable-plans

## 4. Miro for Nonprofits — 30% off any plan (stacks to ~50%)
- **What you get:** 30% off any paid Miro plan, combinable with the standard 20% annual discount (~50% total). Useful for planning/whiteboarding across seminary + Plumbline.
- **Eligibility/validation:** Submit documentation proving nonprofit status (501(c)(3) letter); approval within ~10 days; discount lasts for the life of the account.
- **Source:** https://miro.com/npo/

## 5. HubSpot for Nonprofits — 40% off Pro/Enterprise
- **What you get:** 40% off net-new Professional or Enterprise HubSpot products (marketing/CRM/email). Only worth it over the already-surfaced Salesforce Power of Us donation if you prefer HubSpot's marketing stack.
- **Eligibility/validation:** Registered North America nonprofit, **new** HubSpot customer, annual contract required; apply through HubSpot sales (mention the Nonprofit Program). Starter tier excluded; can't stack with other discounts.
- **Source:** https://www.hubspot.com/nonprofits
