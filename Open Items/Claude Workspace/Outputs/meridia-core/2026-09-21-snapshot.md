## Failure classification

Literal symptom is `websocket: bad handshake`, but a direct edge probe narrows it past the service-token hypothesis:

| Probe | Result |
|---|---|
| `curl https://db.meridiahq.com` WITHOUT CF-Access headers | **HTTP 403** (Access correctly blocking) |
| `curl https://db.meridiahq.com` WITH CF-Access service-token headers | **HTTP 502** (auth accepted; origin unreachable) |
| DNS | resolves to Cloudflare edge (2606:4700::/32) — propagation OK |

The 403→502 flip proves the **service token is valid** and Cloudflare Access is admitting the request. The 502 means the Cloudflare edge cannot reach the tunnel origin: **the NAS-side cloudflared tunnel connector for db.meridiahq.com is down or disconnected** (container stopped, tunnel not running, or cloudflared-in-Docker unable to reach 192.168.0.160:5433).
