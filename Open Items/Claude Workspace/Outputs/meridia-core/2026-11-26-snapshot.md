**Likely causes, in order:**
1. The tunnel's public-hostname route for db.meridiahq.com is missing, or isn't a `tcp://` service. A route set to an http(s) service, or one pointing to a dead origin, gives a 502 and refuses the websocket upgrade.

3. The connector is down or unhealthy. That usually shows as 530/1033, so it's less likely given the 502.
