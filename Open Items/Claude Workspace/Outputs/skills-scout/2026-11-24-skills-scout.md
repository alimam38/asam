## Aegis (governance / audit for agents)
2. **CSA RiskRubric v2 is live: free risk scorecards for 203 MCP servers and 44 AI models.** The Cloud Security Alliance's nonprofit AI foundation launched v2 on Nov 23 at riskrubric.ai. Each rating has a 0–1000 composite score, an A–F grade, a breakdown by pillar, and the evidence from each scanner. MCP servers are scored on Security (40%), Transparency, Reliability and Privacy (20% each). Models add a new "Excessive Agency" pillar: does the system stay inside its goals, permissions and boundaries when pushed. Three independent scanners (PointGuard AI, Deloitte Italy, Tumeryk) score each item, disagreements are shown side by side, and the methodology is published. No sign-up, no fee. MCP server scores range from 170 to 995. *Why it matters:* (a) when you vet a new connector, check its RiskRubric score alongside the MCP Registry malware check (surfaced 11-23). (b) For Aegis, the pillar design, failure-weighted scoring (one critical failure is not averaged away), confidence index and retained test evidence are a ready reference for governance-alert approvals and audit trails. CSA says plainly that this is a first screen, not a full evaluation. *Trust:* Tier 2. CSA is an established standards body (not in the sources file); the methodology is public. — cloudsecurityalliance.org/blog/2026/11/23/riskrubric-v2-is-live-know-the-risk-before-you-deploy · riskrubric.ai
