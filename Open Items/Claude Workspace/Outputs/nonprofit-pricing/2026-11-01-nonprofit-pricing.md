## 1. Claude Corps (Anthropic) — host a fully funded AI fellow + $10k grant
- **What you get:** A 12-month, fully funded early-career AI fellow placed in your org (employed and paid by CodePath; Anthropic funds the program) plus a **$10,000 Host Implementation Grant**. Effectively a free full-time AI builder for a scoped seminary project (e.g., Populi/ops automation).
- **Eligibility/validation:** Host applications are rolling for the **Jan 2027 and Aug 2027** cohorts. Needs a scoped AI project. ⚠️ Public pages don't state host criteria — the FAQ raises universities and religious/education orgs without answers. Confirm fit with hosts@claudecorps.org before investing in the application.
- **Source:** https://www.anthropic.com/claude-corps/host · https://thenonprofittimes.com/npt_articles/anthropic-launches-claude-corps-with-150m-pledge/

## 2. Human-I-T Non-Profit Gold Membership — refurb laptops 40–60% off
- **What you get:** Free membership; certified-refurbished Apple, Lenovo, Dell, and HP laptops at 40–60% below retail, bulk discounts, payment plans, **1-year warranty** + tech support (25-point certification). Closes the open hardware item from 2026-06-23 (Human-I-T).
- **Eligibility/validation:** 501(c)(3). Upload IRS determination letter, state registration, or current Form 990; approval typically 1–3 business days.
- **Source:** https://www.human-i-t.org/buying-laptops-for-nonprofits/

## 3. Gemini app + Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM) — free in all Workspace for Education editions
- **What you get:** Gemini app and Gemini Notebook included at no cost in every Google Workspace for Education edition, as Core services under the Education Terms (no human review, data not used to train models, FERPA/COPPA support). Changed since the 06-23 Workspace Fundamentals entry — AI tools for faculty/students without buying Gemini add-ons.
- **Eligibility/validation:** Requires a Workspace for Education domain (accredited-institution verification by Google). Admin turns each app on in the Admin console (by OU/group). Confirm availability in Fundamentals in your console.
- **Source:** https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/getting-started/editions/quickstart-guide-to-gemini-and-gemini-notebook-for-education

## 4. Buffer for Nonprofits — 50% off
- **What you get:** 50% off Buffer (social scheduling/analytics) for seminary comms.
- **Eligibility/validation:** Nonprofit charity orgs; create a free account, then submit the form at buffer.com/nonprofits with your documentation (501(c)(3) letter). Reply within ~48 hours. Plan coverage isn't specified — confirm before paying.
- **Source:** https://support.buffer.com/articles/buffers-nonprofit-discount-5DWYhyusYn

## 5. Zapier for Nonprofits — 15% off any paid plan
- **What you get:** 15% off any paid Zapier plan (excludes pay-per-task charges); a separate 33% discount applies to annual prepay. Useful glue for Populi/Gmail/Sheets automations.
- **Eligibility/validation:** Upload proof of charitable status (501(c)(3) letter) via zapier.com/non-profits → "Apply for discount"; applied at checkout after approval. Tax exemption is requested separately.
- **Source:** https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496197165581-Discounts-for-Zapier-plans
