## 1. Robert W. Woodruff Foundation — Education / one-time & capital grants  ★ strongest fit (Phase III)
- **What it funds:** Atlanta's largest local funder. Backs *one-time needs* (not operating) across Education, Community Development, Health, etc. — the exact profile for a capital campaign. Prefers organizations with a proven track record and enduring/capital projects.
- **Deadline:** Two cycles a year — **Feb 1** (April board) and **Aug 15** (Nov board). The Aug 15, 2026 cycle just closed, so the next window is **2026-11 → 2027-02-01**. An informal eligibility inquiry must be submitted *before* a full application.
- **Eligibility + verify:** GA 501(c)(3) public charity, paid full-time staff, annual budget **> $500K**. **Does not fund churches.** VERIFY: confirm with fdns@woodruff.org / 404.522.6755 that a degree-granting theological seminary is treated as an eligible "Education" higher-ed institution (vs. excluded as a religious org), and that Phase III facilities qualify as a fundable one-time need. Submit the pre-inquiry as soon as the Aug-cycle results clear.
- **Source:** https://www.woodruff.org/grants/how-to-apply

## 2. Trinity Church Wall Street — Leadership Development grants (faith-based leaders)

VERIFY: that Turner's intended project (e.g., ministry-leadership / program development) maps to a *current* Leadership Development priority, and confirm domestic theological-education projects are in scope this cycle — their explicit seminary funding is largely Anglican-Communion international, so pre-screen via the quiz before investing in a full proposal.
- **Source:** https://trinitychurchnyc.org/community/how-we-give/grants

## 3. NEH — Spotlight on Humanities in Higher Education  (watch / eligibility check)

- **Deadline:** Next deadline / application-open dates **not yet posted** (program is between cycles). Watch the NEH Education Programs NOFO for the reopened round.
- **Eligibility + verify:** VERIFY the key open question — the program historically targets *undergraduate* humanities teaching and does not name graduate/theological institutions; confirm graduate-only seminary eligibility with NEH Division of Education Programs (202-606-8471) before building anything.
- **Source:** https://www.neh.gov/program/spotlight-humanities-higher-education
