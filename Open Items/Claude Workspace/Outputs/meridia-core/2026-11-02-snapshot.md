## Classification
The client's "bad handshake" is a symptom, not the root cause. The service token gets past Access (403 → 502), and DNS resolves to Cloudflare. The 502 (rather than 530/1033) means the tunnel is registered but its connector cannot dial the TCP origin at 192.168.0.160:5433. Most likely cause: cloudflared on the NAS runs in Docker without host networking, or Postgres is not listening or bound on 5433.

## Fix (Ali)
On the NAS, check the cloudflared container's network mode (`network_mode: host`, or point the ingress at a reachable address). Confirm Postgres listens on 192.168.0.160:5433. Check the tunnel's ingress rule `service: tcp://192.168.0.160:5433`.
