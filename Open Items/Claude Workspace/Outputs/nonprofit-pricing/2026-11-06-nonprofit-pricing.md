## 1. Blackbaud "First Year Free": fundraising research and analytics, ⏰ ends Dec 31 2026
- **What you get:** The subscription fee is waived for the first contract year of ResearchPoint (prospect research), ProspectPoint Predictive Modeling, Direct Marketing Analytics, and Identity Thread. Fits Elevation Campaign donor research. Later years are billed at the rates on your Order Form.
- **Eligibility/validation:** Open to new and existing Blackbaud customers, but only for **new solution purchases** (not renewals or add-ons). The org must be US-registered. You sign a new agreement or amendment. It can't be combined with other discounts, and implementation, training, and services are excluded. Book a discovery call to confirm eligibility. ⚠️ Read the multi-year price on the Order Form before you sign, because year 2 is full price.
- **Source:** https://www.blackbaud.com/nonprofit-first-year-free-promo (page updated 2026-09-24)

## 2. Microsoft 365 Copilot ($25.50/user/mo) and Power BI Pro ($3/user/mo) at nonprofit pricing
- **What you get:** Copilot is 15% off commercial pricing at $25.50/user/mo (annual) and needs a qualifying M365 plan. Power BI Pro is $3/user/mo for BI and dashboards, and Power BI Desktop is free. Earlier digests covered the M365 Basic and Premium grants but not these two line items. Source: Microsoft's offers guide, published Sept 2026.
- **Eligibility/validation:** You register and verify in the Microsoft Nonprofit Hub (501(c)(3)). ⚠️ Microsoft's nonprofit policy has historically excluded schools, colleges, and universities and sent them to Microsoft Education pricing instead. Confirm in the Hub that the seminary was accepted under the nonprofit track. If it was refused, compare the Microsoft 365 Education (A-plan) Copilot pricing.
- **Source:** https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/nonprofits/empower-your-nonprofit-with-AI · https://microsoft.com/en-us/nonprofits/offers-for-nonprofits

## 3. JetBrains IDEs: 50% off for accredited institutions and free classroom licenses
- **What you get:** 50% off commercial subscriptions (IntelliJ, DataGrip for Postgres, All Products Pack) for internal development at universities and accredited educational institutions. A separate nonprofit track gives 25–50% off on a sales quote. All tools are **free** in classrooms and labs for teaching.
- **Eligibility/validation:** The education discount needs proof of accreditation and covers internal projects only. The nonprofit discount needs a 501(c)(3) and a quote from sales. ⚠️ Plumbline work only qualifies if it's a seminary-internal project. Commercial product development would need the separate startup discount.
- **Source:** https://www.jetbrains.com/store/ (Special Offers section)

## 4. Constant Contact: nonprofit prepay discount (20% for 6 months / 30% for 12 months)
- **What you get:** 20% off a 6-month prepay or 30% off a 12-month prepay for email and SMS marketing. Monthly billing gets no discount.
- **Eligibility/validation:** Send the **IRS Letter of Determination**; Tax ID paperwork alone doesn't count. **Don't buy a prepay plan until the nonprofit pricing is confirmed.** New customers call 866-511-1966. Existing customers email their paperwork to billing with "ATTN: Nonprofit Paperwork" in the subject line. Tax exemption is handled separately.
- **Source:** https://knowledgebase.constantcontact.com/email-digital-marketing/articles/KnowledgeBase/5374-non-profit-discount-and-sales-tax-exemption-information (updated 2026-08-21)

## 5. Mailchimp: 15% off for verified nonprofits
- **What you get:** 15% off Mailchimp marketing plans. The help page lists no exclusions for religious or educational orgs.
- **Eligibility/validation:** Add the seminary's EIN to the account, then request the discount through Contact → Billing → Non-profit Discount Requests **before** buying a plan. Discounts aren't applied retroactively. Billing may ask for the 501(c)(3) letter. Pick either this or Constant Contact (#4), not both.
- **Source:** https://mailchimp.com/help/about-nonprofit-discount/
